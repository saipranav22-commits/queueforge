### Comparison Matrix

| Visual Dimension | Phase 13A / 13C Baseline | Phase 13E Midnight Spectrum | Why This Fixes Dissatisfaction |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Canvas Background** | Pure flat dark `#09090b` or `#0e0e11` | Midnight Ink `#0B0B18` with subtle dual radial atmosphere glow | Eliminates the lifeless "void" feel while avoiding intrusive textures. |
| **Surface Elevations** | Uniform flat dark rectangular cards (`#18181b` / `#1c1917`) | Deep Indigo (`#19182F`) to Plum Graphite (`#24213B`) with 145° subtle gradient and 1px inset highlight | Cards now appear tactile, distinct, and layered in physical space. |
| **Card Borders** | High-contrast or invisible grey (`#27272a` / `#3f3f46`) | Soft Lavender-tinted `#393451` with interactive hover states (`#524B73`) | Borders define components cleanly without creating harsh, jarring visual wireframes. |
| **Primary Brand Accent** | Fluorescent Cyan (`#06b6d4`) or Understated Purple | Royal Violet (`#7955F6`) paired with luminous Electric Iris (`#A78BFA`) | Delivers authoritative, premium developer-tool aesthetic reminiscent of Linear and Raycast. |
| **Telemetry Rail** | Monochromatic grey bars with small metric text | High-density 5-metric rail with distinct accent color coding: Gold, Iris, Emerald, Blue, Coral | Instant optical differentiation between queue backlog, worker leases, fleet health, and DLQ. |
| **System Topology** | Flat dark canvas with thin grey boundary rectangles | Deep Navy/Purple architectural zones, gradient container nodes, glowing connection pathways | Converts the 7-service distributed architecture into a centerpiece interactive diagram. |
| **Light Theme** | Conventional generic white / grey office theme | Executive Pearl (`#F6F5FA`) with crisp white surfaces and lavender undertones | Light mode feels intentionally crafted rather than an inverted afterthought. |
| **Unlock Console Action** | Inconsistent size or faded button | Guaranteed $\ge 44 \times 44\text{px}$ target, Royal Violet token styling, glowing drop shadow | Remains impossible to miss while harmonizing with the overarching control plane. |
