# QUEUEFORGE COMMAND — PHASE 13F VISUAL DIRECTION COMPARISON
## PROTOTYPE AUDIT & EVALUATION REPORT

---

### 1. Prototype Overview

To validate the **TITANIUM × EMBER** aesthetic before universal application, two browser-rendered visual prototypes of Mission Control were constructed and captured at 1440 × 900:

* **Prototype A — PRECISION COMMAND** (`prototype_a_precision_command_1440x900.png`)
  * Layout: Asymmetric 65/35 grid with integrated calibrated telemetry ruler and champagne brass corner accents.
  * Typography: Large monospaced high-contrast numerals (`QUEUEFORGE / COMMAND 01`), micro-tick borders.
  * Character: Precision Swiss instrument meets high-performance engineering terminal.

* **Prototype B — INDUSTRIAL COCKPIT** (`prototype_b_industrial_cockpit_1440x900.png`)
  * Layout: Chassis bay with milled fastener corner accents, annunciator status panel, and segmented 8-step LED bar gauges.
  * Character: Heavy aerospace and industrial console with physical tactile cues.

---

### 2. Objective Evaluation Matrix

| Criteria | Direction A: Precision Command | Direction B: Industrial Cockpit | Winner & Rationale |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Clearer Hierarchy** | Exceptional. The eye travels logically from the top brand status to the telemetry ruler, down to the topology and subsystems. | Good, but the annunciator bank and heavy chassis frame create competing optical centers. | **Direction A** |
| **Distinctive Identity** | High-end luxury engineering aesthetic that feels bespoke, expensive, and dignified. | Industrial aerospace feel is bold, but borders on heavy-equipment simulation. | **Direction A** |
| **Readability** | High-contrast Warm Ivory (`#F0EDE5`) numbers against deep graphite (`#17191B`) give instant legibility. | Segmented LEDs are visually compelling but require slightly more cognitive effort to decode exact counts. | **Direction A** |
| **Anti-Template Feel** | Replaces 5 isolated card boxes with a continuous calibrated measurement strip with dividing rules. | Strong paneling, though individual segmented gauge blocks still resemble cards. | **Direction A** |
| **Operational Usability** | Immediate comprehension of cluster pressure, lease occupancy, and poison faults in one unified glance. | High tactile satisfaction, great status annunciator cues. | **Tie / Direction A** |
| **Visual Impact** | Subtle brass hairpins, clean graphite plates, and glowing Ember actions deliver an executive command feel. | Strong mechanical punch. | **Direction A** |

---

### 3. Selection Decision

**Direction A: PRECISION COMMAND** was selected as the foundational architecture for Phase 13F, incorporating tactile elements (such as calibrated segmented indicator fills and clean annunciator-style health indicators) into the core components. Both prototype screenshots are preserved in `frontend/visual-review/phase13f/prototypes/` for owner visual review.
